# Cloud First and Digital Procurement Policy

## Cloud first principle

Public bodies must consider public cloud services first when buying or building new digital services. An on-premises solution may be chosen only when a documented options appraisal shows that cloud hosting cannot meet security, cost or legal requirements.

## Approved cloud providers

Only cloud providers listed on the Government Cloud Register may host government workloads. To be listed, a provider must hold ISO 27001 certification, operate at least one region inside Veloria and pass an annual security assessment by the National Cyber Centre.

## Procurement thresholds

Digital contracts with a total value below 50,000 Velorian crowns may be awarded directly with three written quotes. Contracts from 50,000 up to 250,000 crowns require a mini-competition on the Digital Marketplace framework. Contracts above 250,000 crowns require a full open tender published on the national procurement portal.

## Contract length

Cloud hosting contracts may run for a maximum of 4 years, including extensions. Every contract must include an exit plan that allows data to be exported in an open, machine-readable format.

## Spend controls

Any digital project with an expected lifetime cost above 1 million crowns must be approved by the Digital Investment Board before procurement starts.
