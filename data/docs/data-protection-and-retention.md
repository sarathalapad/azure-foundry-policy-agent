# Public Sector Data Protection and Retention Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to every ministry, agency and contractor that processes personal data on behalf of the Government of Veloria.

## Retention periods

Citizen case records must be retained for 7 years after the case is closed and then securely destroyed. Website access logs are kept for 12 months. Recordings of calls to government helplines are kept for 90 days unless they are needed for an open complaint.

## Subject access requests

Any person may ask a public body for a copy of the personal data it holds about them. The public body must respond within 30 calendar days. The deadline may be extended once by a further 30 days for complex requests, and the requester must be told the reason for the extension. Subject access requests are free of charge.

## Data breach notification

A public body that discovers a personal data breach must notify the Velorian Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of it. Where the breach is likely to cause a high risk to individuals, the affected people must also be informed without undue delay.

## Data residency

Personal data classified as Official or higher must be stored and processed in data centres located within Veloria. Transfers outside Veloria require written approval from the Chief Data Officer and a documented transfer impact assessment.

## Encryption

All personal data must be encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and encrypted at rest. Encryption keys for data classified as Secret must be held in a hardware security module controlled by the government.
