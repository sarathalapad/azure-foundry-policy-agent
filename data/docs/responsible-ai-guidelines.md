# Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence in Public Services

## Principles

Public bodies that use artificial intelligence must be transparent, accountable and fair. Citizens must be told when they are interacting with an AI system rather than a person.

## Algorithmic impact assessment

An algorithmic impact assessment must be completed before any AI system that influences decisions about individuals is deployed. The assessment must be published on the Government AI Register within 30 days of the system going live.

## Human oversight

AI systems must not make final decisions on benefits, immigration status or licence applications. A trained official must review and approve every such decision. Citizens have the right to request a human review of any decision that was supported by AI.

## Generative AI tools

Civil servants may use approved generative AI tools for drafting and summarising. They must not enter data classified as Official-Sensitive or higher into tools that are not on the approved list. All generated content must be checked by a person before it is published.

## Monitoring

Every AI system in production must be monitored for accuracy, bias and harmful outputs. Serious incidents must be reported to the Ministry of Digital Services within 5 working days.
