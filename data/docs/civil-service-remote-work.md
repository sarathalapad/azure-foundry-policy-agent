# Civil Service Hybrid and Remote Working Policy

## Hybrid working pattern

Civil servants in eligible roles may work remotely for up to 3 days per week. At least 2 days per week must be spent at an official government workplace, unless a reasonable adjustment has been agreed.

## Eligibility

Staff become eligible for hybrid working after completing a 3 month probation period. Roles that require physical presence, such as front-desk services at Citizen Service Centres, are not eligible.

## Equipment and allowance

The employing department provides a laptop, headset and monitor. Eligible staff may also claim a one-off home office allowance of 400 Velorian crowns for a desk or chair.

## Working abroad

Working from outside Veloria is permitted for a maximum of 10 working days per calendar year and requires prior approval from the line manager and the departmental security officer. Staff must not access data classified as Secret while outside Veloria.

## Security

Remote work must be carried out on government-issued devices connected through the government VPN. Using personal devices to access government email is not permitted.
