# Government Web Accessibility Standard

## Required standard

All public-facing government websites and mobile apps must meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. New services must pass an accessibility audit before they go live.

## Accessibility statements

Every service must publish an accessibility statement that lists known issues and explains how users can request information in an alternative format. The statement must be reviewed at least once every 12 months.

## Languages

Core government services must be available in both official languages of Veloria, Velorian and English, at launch.

## Complaints

Users may report an accessibility problem through the feedback link on any government page. The responsible public body must acknowledge the report within 3 working days and resolve it, or explain why it cannot, within 20 working days. Unresolved complaints may be escalated to the Digital Services Ombudsman.

## Testing

Automated accessibility testing must run on every release. Manual testing with assistive technologies, including at least one screen reader, must take place at least twice a year.
