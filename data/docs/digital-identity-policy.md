# VeloID Digital Identity Policy

## Purpose

VeloID is the national digital identity credential that residents use to sign in to government online services. This policy sets out who may hold a VeloID, how it is issued and renewed, and how identity data is protected.

## Eligibility

Any Velorian citizen or permanent resident aged 16 or over may apply for a VeloID. Applicants under 16 may be linked to a parent or legal guardian's VeloID as a dependant, but cannot hold their own credential. Temporary visa holders may apply for a VeloID Lite credential, which gives access to tax and health services only.

## Issuance and verification

Applications are made through the VeloID mobile app or at any Citizen Service Centre. Identity is verified against the national population register and one photographic document. A standard application is processed within 5 working days. Applicants who cannot use a smartphone may request a physical smart card at no cost.

## Validity and renewal

A VeloID credential is valid for 5 years from the date of issue. Holders receive a renewal reminder 60 days before expiry. Renewal can be completed online if the holder's photograph is less than 10 years old; otherwise an in-person visit is required.

## Biometric data

Facial images captured during verification are used only to confirm the applicant's identity. The raw biometric template is deleted within 90 days of a successful verification. Biometric data is never shared with law enforcement agencies without a court order.

## Lost or compromised credentials

A holder who suspects their VeloID has been compromised must revoke it immediately through the app or by calling the VeloID helpline, which operates 24 hours a day. A replacement credential is issued within 2 working days of revocation.
